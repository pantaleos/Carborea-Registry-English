# Welcome

The Carborea S.B. S.r.l. Registry contains all certified information relating to reforestation and afforestation projects that, through their management and the application of improved agricultural practices (carbon farming), contribute to the removal and storage of CO₂.&#x20;

The Registry also provides a knowledge base on Carborea's proprietary methodology and standard, as well as references to the international standards adopted. It offers a Q\&A section dedicated to the most frequently asked questions and an in-depth area supporting farmers, companies and ordinary citizens, guiding them in understanding the validation, monitoring, reporting and verification process for projects.
